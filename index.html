Optional · read-only

Understand a lab report, in plain language

Upload a photo of a blood test or lab panel and Vitals will read the values it can recognize and explain, in general terms, what each marker relates to. It does not name a condition, a cause, or a cure — that reading always needs a clinician who has your full history.

Upload a photo of your lab report

JPG or PNG. Processed entirely in your browser — the image is never uploaded anywhere.

This reader only recognizes common markers and typical adult reference ranges — it does not know your age, sex, medications, or history, all of which change what a value means. It never identifies a condition, a cause, or a treatment. Please share the original report with a doctor or your lab for an actual interpretation.
